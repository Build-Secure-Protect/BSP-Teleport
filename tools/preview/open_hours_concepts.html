Open hours for portals and warps

A portal in Real clock mode can open for several stretches a day instead of one, and a warp gets a new TIME rule with the same list of stretches. The example is the owner's: open 15:00 to 17:00 and 03:00 to 05:00, server zone Europe/London. Each option shows the three places the change appears: the portal's Open/close tab, the admin panel's rules for one warp, and the warp's row on the band (and in a portal's chooser card). Use the toggles to move the clock and to see a player's read-only view.

What stays the same in all three

  • Up to 4 windows per portal and per TIME rule. A window may cross midnight (22:00 to 02:00). Windows that touch or overlap are joined when saved.
  • Old Real clock portals keep their single window as window 1.
  • A player who tries a closed warp is told "Market is closed. It opens at 03:00 (Europe/London)." Admins pass, as with every rule.
  • Commands: /bsptp portal mode realclock 15:00-17:00 03:00-05:00 and /bsptp warp rule <warp> add time 15:00-17:00 03:00-05:00.
