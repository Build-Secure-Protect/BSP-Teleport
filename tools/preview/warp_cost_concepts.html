Setting a warp's cost on the admin panel

Today the WARPS tab does have cost keys: the two small keys with a minus and a plus. They have no label, move the cost in steps of 10, and nothing on the row shows the cost while it is 0. All three options below can be tried: press the keys.

Set warp here
Cost of market coins, 0 is free
  • The key always shows the cost, or FREE, so it is easy to find.
  • Press it and the bottom strip asks for the amount. Type any number. Enter saves.
  • Replaces the minus and plus keys. The name keeps its room (about 16 characters).

  • Rules becomes Edit, and cost sits above the rules on the same screen.
  • The row is the tidiest of the three: the name gets about 23 characters of room.
  • Cost takes two presses to reach, and the row shows it only as small text once it is above 0.

  • The smallest change: today's keys, gold, with the amount between them.
  • Steps of 10, or 100 with Shift held. No typing, so 25 needs the command.
  • The name loses room, to about 13 characters, and warp names may be 16.
