Portal Concepts

Portals for BSP-Teleport: an admin-only Portal Core and Portal Frame blocks make a multiblock of any shape in one vertical plane; with a destination the opening fills with a coloured field, and the portal opens and closes by hand, by redstone or on a clock with hangar doors sliding in from both sides. Three jobs on this page, three options each: the look of the blocks, the core screen, and the PORTALS tab on the admin panel. The controls below drive every mock-up at once.

State:

1. The blocks

Each option shows the four textures at 16 by 16 (frame, core, door body, the door edge that meets the other door) and a 9 by 6 portal front with a 7 by 4 opening and the core in the bottom row. The Colour control tints the field; option C also tints the frame. Door state shows the two door styles from D60: Blocks fills cells from both ends, Rendered slides two panels.

What all three share

  1. Frame and core are full cubes with the same panel language on every face, so any ring shape reads as one machine. The core has one distinct face so an admin can find it; its facing (set when placed, flippable on the screen) is the side players arrive on.
  2. The field block is the Nether portal idea: not solid, light 11, one animated swirl texture tinted by a 24-colour blockstate property (shown in the Colour control). Blocks cannot be placed in it.
  3. Door blocks are solid. On close the field goes at once and the doors fill in from both ends over about half a second; Rendered draws the same doors as sliding panels and still places invisible solid cells so the wall is real.
  4. Unready portals (no destination, open frame, two cores) show an empty frame. Closed portals show doors. Only a ready, open portal shows the field.

2. The core screen

Right-click on the core, admins only (moderators read-only). Same content in all three: a status line, the destination, the look, the open/close mode, and a greyed ACCESS tab for the later system. Each layout is shown on a different part so all of it is visible: A on DESTINATION, B everything at once, C on OPEN/CLOSE.

3. The admin panel PORTALS tab

A new tab on the Map panel from phase 5: every portal on the network with its server, state and destination, and Go to, Open, Close and Lock. The WARPS tab gets a Portal switch per warp (shown under option A). Tick "destination server down" to see a portal whose server has gone away.

Rules the mock-ups assume

  • Shape (D54): frame blocks in one vertical plane, joined by flat faces; the opening is the enclosed air of that plane; the core counts as a frame block; one core per frame; 24 by 24 at most, 300 opening cells at most.
  • Destination (D55): one of Warp, Portal warp, Random (a server), Position, Link (an id shared by exactly two cores, two-way, "arrive only" possible).
  • Destination down (D57): "The portal is not answering" and nothing moves; with "close when the destination is down" on, the doors shut by themselves and reopen when it is back.
  • Modes (D59): Manual, Redstone, Real clock, Game clock, plus Lock over any mode.
  • On use (D61): no warm-up, no band cooldown; 5 s per player per portal; the raid rule and thief block still apply; zone checks skipped; cross-server trips carry totems through BSP-Core.

Picks to make

  1. Block look: A Hangar, B Bulkhead or C Lumen. Default: A.
  2. Core screen: A Tabs, B Board or C Steps. Default: A (same bones as the band and the panel).
  3. PORTALS tab: A List, B By server or C Link map. Default: A.
  4. Colour palette: the 24 in the Colour control (16 dyes plus turquoise, violet, gold, rose, mint, amber, ice, ember). Default: as shown.
  5. Door speed: one cell per side every 2 ticks for Blocks, the same travel time for Rendered. Default: as shown.
