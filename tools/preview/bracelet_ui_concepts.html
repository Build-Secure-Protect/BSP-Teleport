Teleport Bracelet Concepts

The bracelet is the item every player always has: it sits in a Curios slot, stays through death, and opens the player's teleport screen. Section 1 shows three looks for the item. Section 2 shows three layouts for the screen, drawn in the Holo HUD style, with the same content in each: homes (with locked ones), warps (with costs), players on the network (requests in and out), spawn, back, random, and the cooldown. Pick one of each, or mix.

State:

1. The item: three refinements of the Band

Drawn at 16 x 16, the item's real size. The tiny copy is how it shows in the band slot after the hotbar. Pick A1, A2 or A3; the texture is written by tools/gen_band_assets.py, so a change is one pixel map.

2. The screen

What all three share

  1. Opened by right-clicking the bracelet, or a keybind (default B). The screen is the same whether or not the player carries a totem; the totem only raises the numbers.
  2. Every teleport from the screen goes through the same warm-up and cooldown as the commands. The screen closes when the warm-up starts and the countdown shows on the action bar.
  3. Locked homes stay listed, greyed, with "locked" and the slot count beside them. The slot count comes from config plus the totem's Extra Homes level.
  4. A destination on another server shows its server name in violet. Costs show in gold. Anything refused by the raid rule shows red with the reason.
  5. The incoming request panel appears only while one is open, with ACCEPT and DENY. Requests expire on their own.
  6. Admin tools stay on the admin panel (phase 5), not here.
