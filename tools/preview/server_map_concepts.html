Server map on the band

A new MAP tab between TEAM and MORE. The example is your network as a player standing on BSP-Spawn sees it: the four worlds joined at their borders (every side links, so the grid wraps round north to south and east to west), Spawn reached by warps, an event server reached by a portal, and two servers nothing leads to. Click any server to see its details in the footer. The other tabs do nothing here.

A: Even tiles

  • Every world is the same size square with its name and border size inside.
  • A teal bridge in the gap joins two worlds that share a border. A crossing to the far side of the grid, on any of the four sides, is a half tile that fades out, named like "Frostwarf south". East and west ones read upwards.
  • Servers off the grid float on the right with a dashed line: gold for warps, violet for a portal. NO ROUTE servers sit under them with no line.
  • Gold edge: where you are. A red tag: you need an access token there.

B: Drawn to size

  • Each world is drawn to the size of its border: Stormfall at 10,000 is the biggest, Moongrove at 6,000 the smallest.
  • On your own world a gold dot shows where you stand. Each tile says how many are online.
  • Off-grid servers are smaller tiles with their lines. NO ROUTE servers are one line of text along the bottom.
  • Clicking a server shows how to get there in the footer.

C: Map and routes

  • A smaller map on the left; on the right a list of every server and its border crossings, written out.
  • Clicking a row or a tile highlights both, and dims everything not linked to it.
  • Easiest to read for a big network; the map itself is the least detailed of the three.
  • The list scrolls like the other tabs when there are more than 9 servers.
