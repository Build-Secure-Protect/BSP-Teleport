Teleporting to allies

BSP-Core is adding alliances of up to three teams. Allies get the same teleports as teammates, except the ally is asked first and has 30 seconds to accept. The band's tab row is already full: with the ADMIN tab, six tabs take its whole width, so a seventh tab cannot simply be added. Each option solves that differently. Press ASK to see what the asker gets.

A: A switch inside TEAM

  • The tab row stays as it is. The TEAM tab gets a two-way switch at the top: MY TEAM and ALLIES.
  • The ALLIES side lists each ally with their team's name in its colour beside the player.
  • The switch only shows when the team is in an alliance.

B: Its own ALLIES tab

  • ALLIES sits beside TEAM, as the request asks. To make room, ADMIN leaves the tab row and becomes a violet "Admin panel" row on the MORE tab (admins only). Every player gets six narrower tabs and no title, as admins have today.
  • Each allied team is a header row in its colour, with its players under it.
  • The tab shows for everyone, and says "Your team is in no alliance" when there is none.

C: One list on TEAM

  • No new tab or switch. The TEAM tab lists your team first, then each allied team under a header in its colour.
  • Teammates have GO (no question); allies have ASK. Scroll for more.
  • Simplest, but a full alliance of three teams makes a long list.

What the ally sees (every option)

Ally Wren (Blue Herons) wants to come to you. [Accept] [Deny] 30 s

The ask uses the same request as /tpa: the band's request line shows it too, /tpaccept and /tpdeny answer it, and a player who ignores requests is not asked. After an accept it is a normal team teleport: cooldown, warm-up, access, zones, steals.
