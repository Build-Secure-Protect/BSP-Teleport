Paying for warps and portals in pieces or coins

Today every warp cost and every cost rule on a portal is paid in Shatter Coins from the inventory. Each option below adds pieces from the bank. Press the keys to try them.

Coins are Shatter Coin items the player carries. Amounts are in Copper value, so 250c means coins worth 250 Copper.
Pieces are the BSP-Core bank balance. One Copper converts to 1,000 pieces by default, so the same price in pieces is a much bigger number.
