Back portals and portal warm-ups

Three options each for adding a Back destination on the core screen, for the Back row on the chooser card, and for how a portal warm-up counts down. The warm-up setting itself sits under the cooldown on the Open/close tab in every option. Decisions D110 to D125 are at the foot. Colours and sizes are the Holo HUD look at 1.5x, drawn against the screens as built in T69.

4. The rules behind all three

  1. Back goes to the player's /back spot: where they last teleported from, or where they died, whichever is newer. It survives server switches and relogs.
  2. A trip that starts inside a portal saves /back at that portal's arrival spot in front of it, and any landing inside a portal field is marked so the field does not fire until the player walks off (B9). Plain /back gains this too.
  3. No spot, a spot in a world /back excludes, or a server that is down: the player stays and is told why; in the chooser the row is greyed with that reason.
  4. Back checks the portal's own rules and also /back's: Region Guard teleport-in, Anti Totem zones, server access. The band's /back cooldown is not touched.
  5. After the trip /back points at the Back portal, so two trips flip between the two places.
  6. Warm-up: the player may move about inside the field; stepping out, being hurt, dying or logging out cancels and applies the portal cooldown. With the chooser it counts after GO, with the player held still.
  7. Coins and rules are checked when the countdown starts and taken when the move happens. bsp_teleport.bypass.warmup skips it; Swift Travel does not shorten it.

5. Decisions (D110 to D125)

D110 Back is a destination kind in the list; alone it is back mode.
D111 The spot is the shared /back spot, death spots included.
D112 B9: /back saved in front of a portal, and every landing in a field is marked.
D113 Nothing to go back to: stay, a line saying why, the row greyed in the chooser.
D114 Portal rules plus /back's checks; the band's /back cooldown untouched.
