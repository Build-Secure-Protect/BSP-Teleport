Multi-destination portals

Three options for the core screen's DESTINATION tab with the list on the right, and three for the popup a player sees when a portal leads to more than one place. Decisions D94 to D106 are at the foot. Colours and sizes are the Holo HUD look at 1.5x; the real screens draw with the same panel, tabs and keys as the portal screen built in T56.

1. The DESTINATION tab

Every option keeps the status line, the five kind pills and the Arrive-only and Close-when-down switches. The difference is how a pick becomes a list entry and how the list is edited. The list is the same data in all three: up to 8 entries, each a kind, a target and a label the admin may overwrite.

2. The chooser popup

Shown the moment a player steps into a portal with two or more usable destinations. The player is held still on the server; every key but Esc is swallowed; Cancel or Esc applies the portal cooldown. Toggle the states to see how each option shows a destination that is down, one the player may not enter, and a single destination (no popup at all).

A. Banner at the top default

Where to?
Hub East leads to several places. Pick one, or Cancel to step back out.

The same band the access popups use (D89), so players already know the shape. Rows with a GO key; a down row is greyed with its reason; a denied row is red with "needs access to BSP-Wilds (Access Token IX)". The world stays visible underneath, which fits "you are standing in the portal".

One tile per destination with its icon (the warp's icon, a portal's field colour, the server's token for a far server), the label large and the detail small. Handsome with up to six; with eight the tiles shrink and the reason text on a down or denied tile gets tight.

3. The rules behind both

  1. One destination in the list: straight through, no popup, the arrival as today. Two or more usable: the popup. Exactly one usable among several: straight through to it.
  2. A destination is usable when its target exists, its server is up and the player may enter that server. Down and denied entries are shown greyed with the reason, never pressable.
  3. While the popup shows the server keeps the player on the cell they stepped into; the screen swallows every key except Esc; inventory keys do nothing. Damage cancels; 30 s of silence cancels.
  4. Cancel applies the portal's per-player cooldown and the arrival mark, so stepping back out does not fire the field again.
  5. The portal's own rules are checked before the popup; coins and items are taken when a destination is chosen and the move happens.
  6. A portal listed by a multi-destination portal, itself listing only that one, works as a plain two-way link does today.

4. Decisions (D94 to D106)

D94 Any kind in the list: warp, portal warp, portal, random, position; up to 8 (portals.maxDestinations).
D95 Kind Portal replaces Link: one-way, names one portal; existing link pairs convert on first load.
D96 Labels default to the target's own name; the admin may overwrite per entry; used in the popup and "Through <name>".
D97 Down entries greyed with the reason; the popup only when two or more are usable.
D98 Held still on the server; Esc cancels; damage cancels; 30 s cancels; never invulnerable.
D99 Cancel applies the cooldown and the arrival mark.
D100 Rules before the popup, charges on the move; a denied entry shows why in the popup (owner's addition).
D101 READY while one entry is usable; close-when-down only when all are down.
D102 Arrival unchanged.
D103 Panel row "3 destinations: Market, Arena, +1"; one-way arrows on the map.
D104 /bsptp portal dest add|del|label|clear; the old dest forms replace the list.
D105 A destinations column added in place; NBT list; the old single destination converted once.
D106 Mock-ups first; the build after the usage reset of 2026-10-24.
