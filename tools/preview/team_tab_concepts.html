Team Tab Concepts

BSP-Core's Teams line asks for a TEAM tab on the Teleport Band: the player's teammates, where each one is, and a GO key for every online one that runs the same teleport as a bracelet teleport (cooldown, warm-up, zones, access, the raid rule). Three layouts of the same content below, in the Holo HUD style. Pick one.

State:

The TEAM tab

What all three share

  1. The tab sits between PLAYERS and MORE and is always there. In no team it says so and points at BSP-Core's Team screen (the Team key on the inventory).
  2. The team banner names the team in its colour, the leader and the member count. Members are in joining order with a small leader mark.
  3. Three states per teammate: here, on another server (the server name in violet), or offline with "seen 2 h ago", greyed.
  4. GO is drawn only for online teammates. On another server without Redis it is greyed as FAR. Within 150 blocks of a running steal it is red as RAID and refused.
  5. GO runs the same server method as BSP-Core's TELEPORT key: cooldown, warm-up (the screen closes, the countdown shows on the action bar), access, zones, the raid rule; the teammate gets one line "X is teleporting to you."
  6. The tab re-reads every 5 s while open and at once when someone joins, leaves, is kicked, the lead changes or the team disbands.
  7. With six tabs (admins) the keys narrow to 36 px and the title shortens to BAND so nothing overlaps.

The real screen is 248 x 196 at the band's scale; the mocks are drawn twice that size. Everything else on the band (the footer, the help line, the raid warning) is unchanged.
